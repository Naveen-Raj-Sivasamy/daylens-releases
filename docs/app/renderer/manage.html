Tabs & fields

Pick what you want to change. Changes save straight to your connected Sheet — no code, no redeploy.

Tabs

Each tab is one thing your team logs. Add one from a template — Daily status, Leave, Ticket tracker, Weekly meeting — or start from Custom, then adjust its fields any time.

Turn off "Show in app" for a tab nobody should type into, like an archive, a pivot table or a chart. It stays in your Sheet.

For says what a tab is for. A Leave tab feeds "Who's off" and the leave calendar; a Ticket tracker gets the ticket list and board; Holidays (one tab: a Date and a name for each day) marks the team's holidays and isn't counted as anyone's entries. Changing it never touches the tab's entries.

Dropdown & Multiselect Options

Each card is one dropdown list (e.g. "Leave.Type"). Click an option to rename it, ✕ to remove it, or type in "Add option" and press Enter. Then Save.

Field Types

Pick a category, then a tab, to change that tab's fields: what kind each one is, the order they appear on the form (↑ ↓, their columns move in the Sheet too) and which are hidden. A new field is added to the Sheet when you save.

Landing-Screen Categories

Group tabs under a category button, picked from the dropdown below each one. A tab not added to any category still shows, just ungrouped.

App Settings

Your team's Teams channel, the AI digest key, and who "Contact Admin" emails. Report reminders are set on each report in Reports → Scheduled reports; which tabs show in the app is in "Tabs".

Teams channel (report reminders)

Optional. Paste a Teams channel's webhook link to also post each report's reminder there. Ticket trackers post to their own channels (Ticket Groups). Leave blank for email only.

Gemini API key (AI digest)

Free key from aistudio.google.com/apikey (your Google account, no credit card). It powers AI digest; leave it blank to keep that off. Good to know: on Google's free tier, Google may use what's sent to improve its products, and people there may read it, so don't use the free tier for confidential work.

Gemini model

Google renames/retires these more often than you'd expect (a "flash" model getting swapped for a newer one is normal) — if the digest ever starts failing with a 404 "model not found/no longer available" error, the error message itself names the replacement model to use; paste it here, no code change needed.

Leave approval

When on, a leave request goes to the approver the person picks (mark approvers in People → Make approver). It shows as pending until they approve it, and only approved leave counts on the dashboard and in the weekly check-in. Both sides get a pop-up.

Admin contact emails

Who the "Contact Admin" footer (every screen) emails when someone submits an issue — and who gets the monthly backups (Clean-up & backups) and the weekly check-in list. Every submission is also logged on _SupportTickets regardless of whether this list is empty.

Clean-up & backups

Keeps your team's Sheet small and quick. On the 1st of each month, rows older than the period you choose are emailed to your admins as CSV files (they open in Excel or Google Sheets), then removed from the Sheet. Keep that email: it's your backup.

Clean-up needs the latest update of your team's Sheet. An admin can install it in Settings → About Daylens → Update.

Leave and holiday records and reminders are never removed. Ticket trackers only lose closed tickets (Resolved, Closed, Done…); open ones stay however old. A row whose date can't be read for certain stays too.

Monthly full copy

On the 1st of each month, every tab is copied into a new Google Sheet in a “Daylens copies” folder in the Google Drive of the account that runs your team's Sheet. The 3 newest are kept; the oldest goes to Drive's trash (restorable there for 30 days). A copy holds everything the Sheet holds — sign-ins and settings too — and only that account can open it, so keep the copies private, as you do the Sheet. To bring something back, open a copy and paste the rows you need into the Sheet.

The monthly full copy needs the latest update of your team's Sheet. An admin can install it in Settings → About Daylens → Update.

Friday check-in

Once a week, anyone who hasn't added anything that week gets a reminder in Daylens, plus an email if they have one in People. Nobody on leave for the whole week is reminded.

The Friday check-in needs the latest update of your team's Sheet. An admin can install it in Settings → About Daylens → Update.
Every at

Emails come from the Google account that owns your team's Sheet. Google lets a free Gmail account email up to 100 people a day (Google Workspace: 1,500), shared by everything the Sheet sends.

Scheduled reports

Each report emails the tabs and columns you pick to the people you choose — every day, every weekday, once a week, once a month, or on days you choose — with an optional reminder to the team a few hours before it goes out.

Reports and reminders are emailed from the Google account that owns your team's Sheet. Google lets a free Gmail account email up to 100 people a day (Google Workspace: 1,500) — every recipient of every email counts, so a big team on a free account may need Workspace.

Your team's Sheet needs the latest update to send reports every day, every weekday, once a month or on days you choose. Until then, reports can go out once a week. An admin can update it in Settings → About Daylens → Update.

Ticket Groups

Each group posts its tickets (from a Ticket tracker tab) to its own Teams channel — pick the group right on the ticket form. Paste the channel's Incoming Webhook link here.

Data Sources

Every tab saves straight to this Google Sheet by default. Point a tab at "Excel" instead to send its new entries to a Power Automate flow (which then writes them into an actual Excel file on OneDrive/SharePoint) rather than a sheet tab here — that tab still needs to exist on the Sheet so its columns/field types are defined, but its rows live in Excel instead. Existing-entries list/edit/delete isn't available yet for Excel-linked tabs — just adding new entries, for now.
